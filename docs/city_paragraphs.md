## Salvador DJF

Dec–Feb in Salvador has warmed by about 0.15 °C per decade since 1951, roughly 1 °C in all. There is a strong, well-established El Niño effect on temperature: after El Niño conditions in the preceding Sep–Nov, Dec–Feb tends to run warmer, and El Niño accounts for about 20% of the year-to-year ups and downs.
